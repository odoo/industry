What is an Odoo Industry Package?

Odoo Industry packages are ready-to-go databases built by Odoo S.A. for specific business sectors. They come pre-configured with the right apps, settings, and data to save you serious setup time.

If standard Odoo features don't fully cover an industry's unique needs, extra information or capabilities can be added directly to the industry module using Odoo Studio or custom lines of code. (Please check our pricing section to see which plan applies to this module!)

What's Included?

  • Step-by-step guides inside the Knowledge app to walk you through the system and explain how to configure and use your database.

  • Pre-set configurations so you don't have to start from scratch (including the right apps and settings).

  • Optional demo data to help you visualize how your workflows will look.

Pro Tip: Test the package with demo data on a separate test database first. Once you're comfortable with how it works, install a clean version without demo data on your live system.

Three Quick Things to Note:

1

Accounting is separate: It is never preinstalled out of the box, but you can easily add it whenever you're ready.

2

Inventory is kept basic: We purposely keep inventory settings simple at the start. It makes Odoo much easier to learn, and you can always turn on advanced tracking later.

3

Global scope: Industry modules are designed for worldwide use, meaning specific country-based localization needs are not covered out of the box.

Customization

Unified Data Flow

Every stage of the process is now seamlessly interconnected.

  • CRM to Job Position: We have introduced a direct link from CRM opportunities to Job Positions, allowing you to easily create a Job Position straight from an opportunity.

  • Job Position to Sales: Job Positions are now linked to Quotations, Sales Orders, and Invoices, with a streamlined process to generate these directly from the Job Position itself.

Job Position Enhancements

Several new data fields and features have been added to the Job Position module:

  • Job Position Status: Easily identify whether a position is linked to a quotation, ongoing, or closed.

  • Job Position Type: Differentiate between internal and external recruitment.

  • Customer Link: Track exactly which customer the job position is open for.

  • Hiring Contact Team: Keep track of all individuals on the customer's side who should receive potential candidate CVs. This includes an easy way to grant them portal access (see Section 3.3).

  • Kanban View Updates: The Kanban view now displays the total number of CVs sent for a quick overview.

Centralized CV Sharing

We have implemented a new, centralized system for tracking and sending candidate CVs to clients.

Talent & Applicant Adaptations

The applicant tracking features have been improved with the following updates:

  • Shared CV Field: A dedicated field for all documents and resumes that we want to share with the client.

  • Availability Date: A new field to specify when the applicant will be available to start.

  • Employee Link: A direct link to an Employee profile if the applicant is an internal candidate or intern.

  • Kanban View Updates: The applicant information displayed on the Job Position Kanban view has been optimized for better visibility.
