Overview

About the industry

This industry vertical is engineered for non-profit social care operators, emergency housing shelters, and Odoo partners delivering tailored solutions for residential community care management. Traditional generic ERP setups and manual spreadsheets struggle to handle complex socio-demographic tracking, prorated overnight stay calculations, and strict regulatory reporting without creating heavy administrative friction. The module extends core Odoo architecture by linking resident profile records to immutable stay history models and mapping family units. It unifies daily care operations with financial workflows by segregating tax-exempt nightly resident contributions in the Invoicing app and automating prorated overnight stay calculations in Odoo Spreadsheet.

What is an Odoo Industry Package?

Odoo Industry packages are ready-to-go databases built by Odoo S.A. for specific business sectors. They come pre-configured with the right apps, settings, and data to save you serious setup time.

If standard Odoo features don't fully cover an industry's unique needs, extra information or capabilities can be added directly to the industry module using Odoo Studio or custom lines of code. (Please check our pricing section to see which plan applies to this module!)

What's Included?

  • Step-by-step guides inside the Knowledge app to walk you through the system and explain how to configure and use your database.

  • Pre-set configurations so you don't have to start from scratch (including the right apps and settings).

  • Optional demo data to help you visualize how your workflows will look.

Pro Tip: Test the package with demo data on a separate test database first. Once you're comfortable with how it works, install a clean version without demo data on your live system.

Three Quick Things to Note:

1

Accounting is separate: It is never preinstalled out of the box, but you can easily add it whenever you're ready.

2

Inventory is kept basic: We purposely keep inventory settings simple at the start. It makes Odoo much easier to learn, and you can always turn on advanced tracking later.

3

Global scope: Industry modules are designed for worldwide use, meaning specific country-based localization needs are not covered out of the box.

Customization

Resident Profiles & Stay Management

Centralize socio-demographic data while maintaining an immutable, audit-proof history of resident stays.

  • Referral Sources: Track intake pathways such as emergency hotlines, street outreach, or partner NGOs.

  • Admission & Departure Reasons: Capture intake contexts and exit destinations to measure social trajectory.

  • Family Unit Mapping: Define household relationships at entry, including single parents and unaccompanied minors.

Operational & Impact Dashboards

Automate monthly billing justifications and generate comprehensive annual reports for state regulators.

  • Monthly Overnight Reporting: Calculate exact prorated stays automatically, eliminating manual night counting.

  • Annual Impact Summary: Aggregate demographic pyramids, top countries of origin, and capacity occupancy rates.

  • Interactive Audit Mode: Recalculate historical reporting periods on demand using embedded Odoo Spreadsheets.

Official Platform Links

Relate internal resident records directly to external and official databases using dynamic deep links.

  • Pattern-Matching Engine: Leverage server actions to generate dynamic URLs based on internal resident IDs.

  • Institutional Compatibility: Pre-configured URL patterns for major systems like SI-SIAO, Fedasil, and BCSS.

  • Streamlined Navigation: Enable staff to access official state portals in a single click without manual searches.
