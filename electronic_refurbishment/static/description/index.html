Odoo for Electronic Refurbishment

This Odoo industry package wires together every aspect of the device lifecycle into a seamless digital ecosystem. It is designed to handle circular workflows, from generic second-hand procurement to intricate technical grading and variant conversions. The solution connects your entire operation, serial-level inventory tracking, multi-channel sales, and VAT margin scheme accounting. By centralizing these processes, it ensures you can qualify, upgrade, and resell unique hardware with absolute precision and financial clarity.

Discover everything you need to know about this package by visiting the Knowledge App after installing it.

Basics

  • Purchase App: Procure incoming second-hand electronics, manage supplier orders, and accurately record the initial condition or categorization of your newly acquired inventory.
  • Inventory App: Trace serial numbers and utilize conversion tools to transform generic or ungraded items into market-ready, condition-graded stock.
  • Sales / eCommerce App: Showcase available graded inventory, prevent overselling out-of-stock devices, and dynamically adjust pricing based on specific product condition attributes.
  • Accounting App: Manage tax compliance natively by tracking purchase and sales data to automate and calculate the VAT margin scheme for second-hand goods.

Customizations

  • Convert products with ease to capture condition (Technical Module)
  • Instantly create invoice when purchasing supplies

Pricing

This advanced module uses multiple apps and Odoo Studio, but also relies on custom lines of code (such as server actions and computed fields) for extra capabilities.
Its cost follows the Custom plan pricing found at odoo.com/pricing, plus a monthly maintenance fee for the custom code.
