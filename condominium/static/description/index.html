Overview

About the industry

This industry vertical is built for syndics, property owners associations (POA), and Odoo partners looking for an integrated solution to manage multi-owner buildings without complex external software. It unifies technical asset management with co-ownership accounting by tracking lot shares and distribution keys, automating annual budget approvals and call-for-funds, organizing general assemblies, and allocating shared building expenses through analytic accounting directly within the ERP.

What is an Odoo Industry Package?

Odoo Industry packages are ready-to-go databases built by Odoo S.A. for specific business sectors. They come pre-configured with the right apps, settings, and data to save you serious setup time.

If standard Odoo features don't fully cover an industry's unique needs, extra information or capabilities can be added directly to the industry module using Odoo Studio or custom lines of code. (Please check our pricing section to see which plan applies to this module!)

What's Included?

  • Step-by-step guides inside the Knowledge app to walk you through the system and explain how to configure and use your database.

  • Pre-set configurations so you don't have to start from scratch (including the right apps and settings).

  • Optional demo data to help you visualize how your workflows will look.

Pro Tip: Test the package with demo data on a separate test database first. Once you're comfortable with how it works, install a clean version without demo data on your live system.

Three Quick Things to Note:

1

Accounting is separate: It is never preinstalled out of the box, but you can easily add it whenever you're ready.

2

Inventory is kept basic: We purposely keep inventory settings simple at the start. It makes Odoo much easier to learn, and you can always turn on advanced tracking later.

3

Global scope: Industry modules are designed for worldwide use, meaning specific country-based localization needs are not covered out of the box.

Customization

Co-Ownership Structural Modeling

Map complex multi-unit building hierarchies, individual lot ownership, and flexible cost-distribution keys within a unified data structure.

  • Hierarchy & Division Management: Structure master building complexes down to individual units, annexes, and parking spaces.

  • Quotas & Share Tracking: Define exact ownership shares per lot to determine voting power and financial liability automatically.

  • Multi-Key Distribution Formulas: Assign custom weightings and distribution keys (e.g., general common charges, elevator usage by floor, heating consumption) directly to properties.

Simple Call-for-Funds Engine

Streamline periodic owner contribution billing based on approved operating budgets and special reserve fund allocations.

  • Budget-Driven Billing: Convert approved annual co-ownership budgets into scheduled quarterly or monthly owner contribution requests.

  • Proportionate Invoice Generation: Automatically calculate and issue individual customer invoices to each co-owner based on their registered lot shares.

Automated Expense Allocation

Distribute incoming vendor invoices and building maintenance bills seamlessly across affected lots using predefined distribution key logic.

  • Rule-Based Bill Splitting: Route vendor invoices to specific cost keys (e.g., roof repair vs. corridor lighting) for instant distribution across co-owners.

  • Analytical Accounting Alignment: Generate balanced analytical moves per lot, ensuring real-time visibility into the financial statement of each co-owner.

  • Year-End Regularization: Compare provisionally billed calls-for-funds against actual allocated expenses to compute and execute year-end balance adjustments.

General Assembly & Meeting Management

Organize co-ownership general assemblies directly through a calendar interface, from agenda preparation to proxy vote tracking and formal minute generation.

  • Integrated Meeting Scheduling: Plan annual and extraordinary general assemblies using an extended calendar view linked directly to the building registry.

  • Quorum & Proxy Management: Register attendance, calculate live voting quorums based on lot shares, and attach digital proxy forms prior to session opening.

  • Resolution & Minute Tracking: Record resolution outcomes per agenda item in real time and automatically log official signed meeting minutes in the building document portal.
