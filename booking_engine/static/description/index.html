Overview

About the technical module

This module introduces a dedicated application to centralize stay offer management directly within Odoo. It cleanly structures accommodation offerings alongside their supporting staff roles and physical resources, while streamlining guest identities, unit allocations, and reservation lifecycles from a unified workspace. Built-in compliance workflows automatically compute local city taxes due to authorities, bridging front-desk guest operations with effortless financial reporting.

What is an Odoo Technical Module?

Developed by Odoo S.A., a Technical Module enhances database functionality beyond standard Odoo. It leverages Odoo Studio and custom Python scripts, meaning installation could impact your licensing fees.

Pricing

Studio (Custom Price) + Lines of Code

This advanced module uses multiple apps and Odoo Studio, but also relies on custom lines of code (such as server actions and computed fields) for extra capabilities.

Customization

Dedicated Accommodation Application

The accommodation app (renamed to match your industry) is the cornerstone of the user experience, bringing scheduling, orders and configuration together in a single workspace.

  • Schedule: A Gantt view of ongoing room assignments provides efficient access to create and edit orders directly from the schedule.

  • Orders: Rental orders are available with native filters for efficient management.

  • Configuration: A dedicated configuration menu puts every model and record needed for setup at your fingertips.

Stay Offers

Stay offers are configured through standard products, enhanced to ease accommodation setup.

  • Product: Stay offer products can be configured seamlessly from the configuration menu of the accommodation app.

  • Planning role: A stay offer product automatically creates an associated planning role to bind resources to.

  • Guests: A specific attribute is used to capture the guest number associated to attribute values.

  • Additional inputs: Additional fields power other capabilities such as city tax and house keeping.

Early Check In & Late Check Out

Early check in and late check out are managed through products and are highlighted in relationship to the order.

  • Product: Early check in and late check out are managed through dedicated products, with a specific accommodation characteristic.

  • Priority flags: When those products are part of an order, a priority flag is displayed to capture this information.

  • Schedule: When those products are part of an order, the associated slots on the schedule display an informative fast backward/forward icon.

  • House keeping: House keeping tasks associated to rooms in early check in and late check out provide an indicator to prioritize tasks efficiently.

City Tax Automation

City tax is managed through automation and server actions, and comes with a widget to ensure billing according to effective stay.

  • Product: City tax is managed through a dedicated product, with a specific accommodation characteristic, and added to a specific section of the order as soon as a sale order line product has city tax.

  • Quantity: The city tax quantity estimate is summed over stay offers based on stay duration and the guest attribute (detailing adults, children and infants).

  • Widget: At checkout, the city tax widget pops up to consolidate the effective stay and update the city tax product line delivered quantity prior to billing.

Guest Identity & Stay Tracking

Guest management is handled by capturing identity details on the contact form as well as in the rental order with a dedicated tab.

  • Identity: Dedicated fields can be filled in during check in to capture guest details.

  • Stays: The guests tab allows capturing the guests of a stay and their assigned room, while displaying an identity check (if identity details are captured on the contact form). This information is summarized on the contact form through smart buttons.

House Keeping Management & Reporting

House keeping aims at enabling the management and reporting of house keeping to support accommodation business.

  • Setting: This capability is activated through a specific setting.

  • Menu & views: The House Keeping menu from the accommodation app provides a board overview of the rooms' state, the house keeping tasks and the house keeper employee planning.

  • Role: Stay offers are assigned a house keeping role so that house keeping tasks are created with this role. While a single role is functional, defining multiple house keeping roles allows siloing tasks by property area to limit downtime during employee displacements.

  • Cleaning points: Each stay offer is assigned cleaning points for stay-over and checkout house keeping. This relative scale allows defining the effort of cleaning tasks across all stay offers.

  • Tasks: A dedicated project takes an employee assignation, a resource to be cleaned and additional information for house keepers such as priority flags, the occupancy of the associated resource and the cleaning expected. Tasks are created through a scheduled action on a daily basis.

  • Assignation: Employees can be manually assigned house keeping tasks, while a setting allows this to be done automatically. When auto assignment is set, a daily assignation runs to set the task employee based on house keeper planning (prorated time), task cleaning points (prorated effort) and employee roles.

  • Approval: A specific setting allows requesting approval from specific users. When unset, tasks move automatically from clean to ready stage.

Steering & Performance Dashboard

Specific key performance indicators are made available through a dedicated menu and a dashboard.

  • Setting: This capability is activated through a specific setting.

  • Availability & occupancy: The accommodation app comes with a steering menu providing Gantt views for availability and occupancy detailed by day and stay offer. The custom model powering those views combines shifts, number of rooms and leaves.

  • Dashboard: The performance dashboard allows monitoring KPIs such as ADR, RevPAR and Pickups over different periods. This leverages custom daily lines breaking down sale order lines per day.

Survey Automation

To monitor customer satisfaction, a survey automation is configured.

  • Setting: This capability is activated through a specific setting.

  • Survey: The pointed survey from the settings can be changed.

  • Automation: On a daily basis, a server action sends the survey to customers a few days after check out.
