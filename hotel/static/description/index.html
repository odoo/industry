Overview

About the industry

This industry vertical is engineered for independent hotel operators, boutique hotel groups, and Odoo partners seeking a native solution to run full-service lodging operations without relying on disconnected legacy Property Management Systems (PMS), standalone POS terminals, or manual night audit spreadsheets. It eliminates the severe operational friction caused by rate parity discrepancies across distribution channels, unbilled guest room charges between F&B or spa outlets and the front desk, delayed room turnover schedules, and the constant lag in consolidated financial reporting. By extending core Odoo relational data models, the module seamlessly embeds room inventory, stay parameters, daily rate structures, and guest profile attributes directly into native Rental, POS, and CRM workflows. Ultimately, it bridges the gap between front-office reception and back-office financial governance, enabling unified folio invoicing, real-time RevPAR tracking, and automated revenue accounting directly within Odoo Rental, POS, Invoicing, and Accounting.

What is an Odoo Industry Package?

Odoo Industry packages are ready-to-go databases built by Odoo S.A. for specific business sectors. They come pre-configured with the right apps, settings, and data to save you serious setup time.

If standard Odoo features don't fully cover an industry's unique needs, extra information or capabilities can be added directly to the industry module using Odoo Studio or custom lines of code. (Please check our pricing section to see which plan applies to this module!)

What's Included?

  • Step-by-step guides inside the Knowledge app to walk you through the system and explain how to configure and use your database.

  • Pre-set configurations so you don't have to start from scratch (including the right apps and settings).

  • Optional demo data to help you visualize how your workflows will look.

Pro Tip: Test the package with demo data on a separate test database first. Once you're comfortable with how it works, install a clean version without demo data on your live system.

Three Quick Things to Note:

1

Accounting is separate: It is never preinstalled out of the box, but you can easily add it whenever you're ready.

2

Inventory is kept basic: We purposely keep inventory settings simple at the start. It makes Odoo much easier to learn, and you can always turn on advanced tracking later.

3

Global scope: Industry modules are designed for worldwide use, meaning specific country-based localization needs are not covered out of the box.

Customization

Interactive Shift Gantt View for Stays

Streamline room allocation and stay scheduling through an interactive Gantt interface that supports direct creation and modification of sales orders.

  • Direct Order Manipulation: Create, update, extend, or shift guest reservations directly within the Gantt view while automatically updating underlying Sales Orders.

  • Visual Resource Scheduling: Drag-and-drop allocations across physical room resources with real-time validation against double-bookings and stay constraints.

  • Contextual Quick-Actions: Access reservation details, check-in statuses, and folio links directly via popover modals on active Gantt stay slots.

Resource-Bound Product Form Customization

Simplify product setup by directly linking physical resources while abstracting backend role and shift bindings from everyday configuration views.

  • Direct Resource Mapping: Bind physical rooms and accommodation units directly on the product form for intuitive inventory management.

  • Role Abstraction: Hide complex role-to-shift technical bindings to present a clean, operational setup tailored for hotel administrators.

  • Automated Variant Sync: Map accommodation attributes directly to sellable product variants and booking rule parameters without technical overhead.

Automated City Tax Engine & Night Calculation Widget

Ensure regulatory tax compliance during check-out using an automated city tax calculation workflow powered by a dedicated stay-night widget.

  • Night Calculation Widget: Automatically compute the exact billable night count directly on the order based on rental period and product guests attribute value.

  • Automated Tax Insertion: Append local city tax lines to the order automatically at check-out to guarantee accurate folio invoicing.

Cross-Outlet POS Room Charge Payment Method

Enable restaurant, bar, and retail outlets to post charges straight onto a guest's active stay order directly from the Point of Sale interface.

  • Dedicated Room Charge Method: Select the checkout payment method in POS to search active stays and instantly route bills.

  • Resource-Referenced Posting: Automatically reference the specific physical room resource on consumption lines for clear, auditable guest folios.

  • Live Stay Verification: Validate active reservation statuses and credit thresholds in real time before approving cross-outlet charge postings.

Daily Shift-Driven Housekeeping Automation

Automate room turnover logistics by dynamically generating daily cleaning tasks and assigning them according to staff shift schedules.

  • Daily Task Generation: Automatically trigger housekeeping tasks every morning based on stayovers, check-ins, and check-out turnovers.

  • Shift-Aware Assignment: Distribute task loads automatically among on-duty staff members aligned with active shift schedules and capacity.

  • Credit-Based Workload Balancing: Assign point values per room type to ensure balanced productivity targets across active housekeeping shifts.

  • Real-Time Readiness Sync: Update room operational states (Dirty, Clean and Ready) on front-desk views instantly upon task completion.

Automated Customer Satisfaction Survey Engine

Capture guest feedback automatically post-checkout to drive continuous quality improvement and safeguard online reputation.

  • Triggered Survey Dispatch: Automatically send personalized satisfaction surveys via email following guest check-out.

Time-Horizon Steering View

Gain visual control over property allocation trends and future room availability across rolling time horizons.

  • Availability Timeline Matrix: Visualize real-time room availability across days, weeks, or months within a centralized steering interface.

  • Capacity Optimization: Identify allocation gaps and high-occupancy dates ahead of time to adjust marketing and pricing strategies.

Executive Yield Analytics & Manager Dashboard

Monitor critical property metrics—including RevPAR, ADR, and Occupancy through native visual dashboard built for yield decision-makers.

  • Key Yield Indicators: Calculate and display RevPAR, Average Daily Rate (ADR), and Occupancy Rate dynamically using total room inventory benchmarks.

  • Performance Tracking Cards: Access real-time operational KPIs, booking pace trends, and sales summaries directly within a tailored management view.
