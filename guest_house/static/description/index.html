Overview

About the industry

This industry vertical is engineered for guest house operators, B&B owners, and Odoo partners seeking a native solution to manage intimate lodging operations without the burden of fragmented point-of-sale systems, manual ledgers, or rigid legacy PMS tools. It eliminates the operational friction caused by administrative check-in overhead, missed revenue from unbilled ancillary services, rate synchronization discrepancies across booking channels, and the constant strain on small teams juggling front-desk hospitality with back-office administration. By extending core Odoo relational data models, the module seamlessly embeds room availability, stay parameters, and guest profiles directly into native Sales, CRM, and Inventory workflows. Ultimately, it bridges the gap between personalized guest reception and rigorous financial governance, enabling unified folio invoicing, automated tax compliance, and real-time performance tracking directly within Odoo Rental, Invoicing, and Accounting.

What is an Odoo Industry Package?

Odoo Industry packages are ready-to-go databases built by Odoo S.A. for specific business sectors. They come pre-configured with the right apps, settings, and data to save you serious setup time.

If standard Odoo features don't fully cover an industry's unique needs, extra information or capabilities can be added directly to the industry module using Odoo Studio or custom lines of code. (Please check our pricing section to see which plan applies to this module!)

What's Included?

  • Step-by-step guides inside the Knowledge app to walk you through the system and explain how to configure and use your database.

  • Pre-set configurations so you don't have to start from scratch (including the right apps and settings).

  • Optional demo data to help you visualize how your workflows will look.

Pro Tip: Test the package with demo data on a separate test database first. Once you're comfortable with how it works, install a clean version without demo data on your live system.

Three Quick Things to Note:

1

Accounting is separate: It is never preinstalled out of the box, but you can easily add it whenever you're ready.

2

Inventory is kept basic: We purposely keep inventory settings simple at the start. It makes Odoo much easier to learn, and you can always turn on advanced tracking later.

3

Global scope: Industry modules are designed for worldwide use, meaning specific country-based localization needs are not covered out of the box.

Customization

Interactive Shift Gantt View for Guest Stays

Streamline room and suite allocation through an interactive Gantt interface that supports direct creation and modification of sales orders for boutique guest houses.

  • Direct Order Manipulation: Create, update, extend, or shift guest reservations directly within the Gantt view while automatically updating underlying Sales Orders.

  • Visual Room Allocation: Drag-and-drop stay allocations across individual guest rooms and suites with real-time validation against double-bookings and stay constraints.

  • Contextual Quick-Actions: Access guest preferences, check-in statuses and folio links directly via popover modals on active Gantt stay slots.

Resource-Bound Product Form Customization

Simplify room setup by directly linking physical guest house rooms and suites while abstracting backend role and shift bindings from everyday configuration views.

  • Direct Resource Mapping: Bind physical guest rooms, private suites, and annex units directly on the product form for intuitive inventory management.

  • Role Abstraction: Hide complex role-to-shift technical bindings to present a clean, operational setup tailored for guest house hosts and innkeepers.

  • Automated Variant Sync: Map room attributes (bed configurations, balcony access, private bathrooms) directly to sellable product variants and booking rules.

Automated Tourist Tax Engine & Night Calculation Widget

Ensure regulatory compliance during check-out using an automated tourist tax calculation workflow powered by a dedicated stay-night and guest count widget.

  • Night & Guest Calculation Widget: Automatically compute the exact billable night count directly on the order based on stay dates and product guest headcount attributes.

  • Automated Tax Insertion: Append local city or tourist tax lines to the order automatically at check-out to guarantee accurate folio invoicing.
