Pharmacy Retail Management System

This system is designed for pharmacy retail companies to efficiently manage the purchase and sale of products. It includes features for lot-wise stock management and tracking the expiry of product lots, ensuring compliance and accuracy.

Pricing

Studio (Custom Price)

This module uses multiple apps and Odoo Studio to set up custom views, fields, and relationships, without any custom lines of code.

Its cost follows the Custom plan pricing found at odoo.com/pricing.
