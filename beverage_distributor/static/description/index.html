Overview

About the industry

This industry vertical is tailored for wholesale beverage distributors, cash-and-carry operators, and Odoo integrators requiring a native ecosystem to manage high-volume inventory logistics and strict compliance rules. Standard supply chain apps often introduce operational friction when handling bulk-to-unit conversions, reusable asset liabilities, and dynamic alcohol tax calculations, frequently forcing businesses into fragile spreadsheet workarounds. Our module seamlessly bridges these technical gaps by embedding specialized auto-splitting Bill of Materials (BoM), excise calculation engines, and deposit bindings directly into core Odoo Sales, Purchase and Inventory workflows. By unifying these operations, it provides a closed-loop wholesale distribution system extending from automated inbound pallet breakdowns and tax liability reporting to omnichannel container return reconciliation.

What is an Odoo Industry Package?

Odoo Industry packages are ready-to-go databases built by Odoo S.A. for specific business sectors. They come pre-configured with the right apps, settings, and data to save you serious setup time.

If standard Odoo features don't fully cover an industry's unique needs, extra information or capabilities can be added directly to the industry module using Odoo Studio or custom lines of code. (Please check our pricing section to see which plan applies to this module!)

What's Included?

  • Step-by-step guides inside the Knowledge app to walk you through the system and explain how to configure and use your database.

  • Pre-set configurations so you don't have to start from scratch (including the right apps and settings).

  • Optional demo data to help you visualize how your workflows will look.

Pro Tip: Test the package with demo data on a separate test database first. Once you're comfortable with how it works, install a clean version without demo data on your live system.

Three Quick Things to Note:

1

Accounting is separate: It is never preinstalled out of the box, but you can easily add it whenever you're ready.

2

Inventory is kept basic: We purposely keep inventory settings simple at the start. It makes Odoo much easier to learn, and you can always turn on advanced tracking later.

3

Global scope: Industry modules are designed for worldwide use, meaning specific country-based localization needs are not covered out of the box.

Customization

Packaging BoMs

Manage intelligent inventory unpacking and unit-of-measure conversions directly within Odoo Inventory workflows.

  • Auto-Splitting Kitting BoMs: Purchase and receive inventory in bulk pallets while the system seamlessly explodes the Bill of Materials to make individual cases, crates, or bottles immediately available for order fulfillment.

Automated Excise Tax Management

Streamline compliance and financial workflows with an integrated excise engine designed specifically for alcohol distribution.

  • Sales & Invoicing Integration: Automatically calculate and inject excise duty lines on wholesale quotes and invoices based on Alcohol by Volume (ABV) metrics, product categories, and destination regions.

  • Authority Reporting: Generate exact tax obligation reports required by customs and local tax authorities from moving inventory, eliminating manual spreadsheet consolidation and compliance risks.

Deposit Management & Auto-BoM Engine

Close the loop on reusable asset tracking with automated tax-based deposit accounting for wholesale logistics.

  • Tax-Based Deposit Automation: Link deposit products to sell-able goods to automatically apply fixed deposit taxes on B2B sales orders, keeping documents clean while exposing deposit totals in distinct columns.

  • Auto-BoM Inventory Splitting: Automatically split parent products (e.g., full pallets or crates) into sub-products (bottles) and "By-Deposit" items (empty kegs or crates). The system auto-generates the underlying BoMs and replenishment rules.

  • Omnichannel Return Routing: Process returns efficiently by validating them directly on incoming receipts to credit the B2B customer, or by processing negative-quantity deposits natively in the PoS for cash-and-carry operations.

  • Centralized Category Configuration: Define dedicated deposit categories that, when assigned to products, automatically configure the correct deposit amounts and underlying accounting taxes for high-volume distribution.
