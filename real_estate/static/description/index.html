Overview

About the industry

This industry vertical is built for real estate agencies and Odoo partners looking for a scalable solution to manage property transactions without cluttering core workflows. It routes owners and buyers opportunities into distinct pipelines, automates buyer-property matchmaking, provides property centric opportunity follow up, and handles multi-party commission structures directly within the ERP.

What is an Odoo Industry Package?

Odoo Industry packages are ready-to-go databases built by Odoo S.A. for specific business sectors. They come pre-configured with the right apps, settings, and data to save you serious setup time.

If standard Odoo features don't fully cover an industry's unique needs, extra information or capabilities can be added directly to the industry module using Odoo Studio or custom lines of code. (Please check our pricing section to see which plan applies to this module!)

What's Included?

  • Step-by-step guides inside the Knowledge app to walk you through the system and explain how to configure and use your database.

  • Pre-set configurations so you don't have to start from scratch (including the right apps and settings).

  • Optional demo data to help you visualize how your workflows will look.

Pro Tip: Test the package with demo data on a separate test database first. Once you're comfortable with how it works, install a clean version without demo data on your live system.

Three Quick Things to Note:

1

Accounting is separate: It is never preinstalled out of the box, but you can easily add it whenever you're ready.

2

Inventory is kept basic: We purposely keep inventory settings simple at the start. It makes Odoo much easier to learn, and you can always turn on advanced tracking later.

3

Global scope: Industry modules are designed for worldwide use, meaning specific country-based localization needs are not covered out of the box.

Pricing

Studio (Custom Price) + Lines of Code

This advanced module uses multiple apps and Odoo Studio, but also relies on custom lines of code (such as server actions and computed fields) for extra capabilities.

Its cost follows the Custom plan pricing found at odoo.com/pricing, plus a monthly maintenance fee for the custom code.

Note: You can view the exact maintenance fee by hovering your mouse over the "i" info icon next to  Lines of code .

Customization

Tailored Property Data Engine

Capture essential technical, regulatory, and financial attributes for every real estate listing within a dedicated property model.

  • Comprehensive Asset Profiling: Store structural metrics, cadastral details, and legal zoning rules directly on the property record.

  • Commercial Catalog: Expose listings on the eCommerce website as commercial ads.

Portal Lead-to-Pipeline Routing

Seamlessly convert inbound listing inquiries into structured sales opportunities within your agent CRM pipelines.

  • External Reference Tracking: Parse incoming inquiries from web contact forms or external portals using listing reference codes to route leads directly to the assigned agent.

  • Property-Centric CRM Tracking: Link opportunities directly to target properties, giving agents complete visibility over lead history, inquiry volume, and prospect interactions.

Automated Buyer Matchmaking

Cross-reference buyer requirements with active property listings to accelerate sales cycles and notify qualified leads instantly.

  • Criteria-Based Matching: Automatically align seeker criteria (budget, location, surface area, amenities) with available inventory in real time.

  • Automated Notifications: Trigger instant email alerts to matched buyers as soon as a fitting listing goes live or changes price.

Self-Service Visit Scheduling

Eliminate scheduling friction by empowering prospects to book property viewings directly through automated calendar links.

  • Direct Booking Integration: Embed dynamic calendar links inside email templates for one-click scheduling.

  • Agent Calendar Synchronization: Prevent double bookings by syncing appointment slots in real time with agent calendars while sending automated reminders to prospects.

Online Offer & Digital Signatures

Streamline deal closing with online offer submissions and legally binding electronic document execution.

  • Digital Offer Shortcuts: Send pre-populated formal offer templates directly from the CRM opportunity with preset conditions and purchase prices.

  • Integrated e-Signatures: Enable buyers and sellers to review and sign purchase agreements online, automatically transitioning the pipeline stage upon completion.

Automated Commission Breakdown

Calculate and distribute agency and agent commissions seamlessly upon deal closure to eliminate financial disputes.

  • Flexible Split Rules: Pre-configure multi-party commission structures using percentage-based rules per agent.
