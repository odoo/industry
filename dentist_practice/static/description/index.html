Dentist Practice

Manage the patient journey and daily practice administration with standard Odoo apps for reception, booking, financial proposals, supplies, purchasing, and equipment.

  • A Practice Desk workspace for reception, finances, supplies, paperwork, and the team.
  • Dental acquisition and treatment-proposal pipelines with stage guidance and activity plans.
  • A new-patient website enquiry form and standard online booking pages.
  • Practitioner appointments, employee schedules, shared calendars, and separate chair reservations.
  • Patient quotations, portal acceptance, deposits, invoices, and reception follow-up.
  • Supply packs, stock checks, manual replenishment, supplier orders, receipts, and lot expiration.
  • Laboratory service orders, equipment maintenance, and restricted administrative paperwork.
  • Fictional patient and operations scenarios with a Knowledge walkthrough.

Review practitioners, calendars, permissions, fees, accounting, suppliers, stock levels, manufacturer expiration dates, and approved forms for the practice. Staff coordinate practitioner, chair, assistant, and reception handoffs through the standard apps.

Clinical charting, imaging, prescriptions, sterilization cycle records, and insurance claims remain separate clinical workflows.
