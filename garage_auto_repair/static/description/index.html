Overview

About the industry

This package is built specifically for garages focused on vehicle maintenance and repairs rather than vehicle sales (a separate industry module can be installed for sales management).

Key Capabilities:

  • Appointment scheduling & online booking

  • Vehicle intervention tracking & repair history

  • Daily task and garage workflow management

  • Seamless quotation and invoicing

What is an Odoo Industry Package?

Odoo Industry packages are ready-to-go databases built by Odoo S.A. for specific business sectors. They come pre-configured with the right apps, settings, and data to save you serious setup time.

If standard Odoo features don't fully cover an industry's unique needs, extra information or capabilities can be added directly to the industry module using Odoo Studio or custom lines of code. (Please check our pricing section to see which plan applies to this module!)

What's Included?

  • Step-by-step guides inside the Knowledge app to walk you through the system and explain how to configure and use your database.

  • Pre-set configurations so you don't have to start from scratch (including the right apps and settings).

  • Optional demo data to help you visualize how your workflows will look.

Pro Tip: Test the package with demo data on a separate test database first. Once you're comfortable with how it works, install a clean version without demo data on your live system.

Three Quick Things to Note:

1

Accounting is separate: It is never preinstalled out of the box, but you can easily add it whenever you're ready.

2

Inventory is kept basic: We purposely keep inventory settings simple at the start. It makes Odoo much easier to learn, and you can always turn on advanced tracking later.

3

Global scope: Industry modules are designed for worldwide use, meaning specific country-based localization needs are not covered out of the box.

Customization

End-to-End Integration Flow

Vehicles, Contacts, and Interventions are seamlessly connected through project tasks to ensure complete information tracking and an effortless workflow.

Technical Information

The license plate and chassis numbers automatically sync from the vehicle profile directly onto all backend records, as well as generated quotations, sales orders, and invoices.

Appointment Workflow

Scheduling appointments is simplified by sending a direct reschedule link rather than a standard booking link. This attaches the appointment directly to the existing customer profile—eliminating duplicate public user profiles and removing the need for customers to create a portal account.

How It Works:

  • Automated Draft Slot: Odoo schedules an initial appointment X days out (where X is set by the Booking Lead Time field on the appointment type).

  • Client Link Delivery: The customer receives an email with a direct reschedule link.

  • Confirmation or Adjustment:

    • If the date works: No further action is required; the time slot is already confirmed.

    • If they need a different time: The customer uses the link to choose a new slot without having to re-enter their email or personal details.

Intervention Management

Tasks are custom-tailored to align seamlessly with your daily garage workflow:

  • Direct Document Creation: Generate sales orders or invoices directly from the task with a single click.

  • Smart Task Prioritization: Tasks are automatically sorted by appointment date to keep your daily schedule organized.

  • Automated Client Notifications: System-generated emails keep customers updated when booking an appointment or when their vehicle is ready for pickup.

  • Vehicle On-Site Tracking: Easily record vehicle check-in and check-out times, with full timestamp traceability logged in the Chatter for legal compliance.
