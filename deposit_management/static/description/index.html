Overview

About the technical module

Deposit Management Module aims at enabling the management of deposit related products.

  • Definition of deposit categories: Create deposit categories and assign deposit products accordingly.

  • Assignation of deposit products: Link deposit products to regular products to capture when they are sold involving a deposit.

  • Accounting for deposit amount: Handle deposits automatically during sales thanks to tax configuration.

  • Return of deposit products: Manage deposit returns seamlessly during deliveries.

  • Auto-BoM breakdown: Automatically split products into sub-products while accounting for deposits.

What is an Odoo Technical Module?

Developed by Odoo S.A, a Technical Module enhances database functionality beyond standard Odoo. It leverages Odoo Studio and custom Python scripts, meaning installation could impact your licensing fees.

Customization

Definition of Deposit Categories and Deposit Products

Deposit categories are product categories with the setting Is a Deposit Category enabled. Deposit products are products with a deposit category assigned.

Assigning a deposit category to a product makes it a deposit product and allows to:

  • Expose deposit amount: Display the deposit amount directly in the deposit product form.

  • Automate deposit taxes: Automatically create and assign deposit taxes to the deposit product based on the deposit amount.

  • Enable assignation: Make the deposit product available for linking to sellable products.

Assignation of Deposit Product

Deposit products can be assigned to sellable products by selecting the deposit product in the Total Deposits product form field.

When a deposit product is assigned to a product:

  • The deposit product tax is automatically applied to the sellable product.

  • If the sellable product uses Auto-BoM contained products, an additional field By-Deposit becomes available to define the deposit product resulting from the product breakdown.

Example explained for a Beer crate:

  • A beer crate contains 24 beer bottles.
  • A beer crate has a deposit for the crate of 2.1€.
  • Each beer bottle has a deposit for the container of 0.1€.
  • As a result, the Total deposits for a sell-able beer crate is 4.5€ (2.1 + 24 × 0.1).
  • Splitting the beer crate leads to an empty beer crate (the By-Deposit product with a deposit of 2.1€) and 24 bottles of beer each having their deposits of 0.1€.

Accounting for Deposit in Sales

Sellable products include a fixed deposit tax when linked to a Total Deposit product.

  • Each sale order line displays the deposit tax amount for clarity.

  • The total deposit taxes are computed and shown under the deposit tax group near the order total.

In other words, once your sellable products are assigned the appropriate deposit products, deposit accounting is handled automatically during sales.

Returning Deposit Products During Deliveries

When delivering products to your customers, proceed to a return and capture the deposit products they return.

Validate the return, which records the returned deposit products in the customer's sale order.

Similarly, from your Point of Sale (PoS), when customers return deposit products, simply select the deposit product and change its sign in the PoS order to account for it as a return.

Breaking Down Products Accounting for Deposits (Auto-BoM)

The Auto-BoM capability facilitates inventory management for products that can be broken down, allowing you to sell sub-products without manual stock handling.

To define such breakdown pattern:

  • On a product form, indicate in the Auto-BoM section the products it's made of.

  • Assign, if needed, the By-Deposit product.

As a result, automation includes:

  • A Bill of Material (BoM) is created to obtain sub-products from the product, and By-Deposit if defined as a by-product.

  • An automated replenishment rule is created for each warehouse to replenish sub-products from the product as soon as needed.

In other words, once sub-products are captured in the Auto-BoM section of your products, stock and product breakdown management are handled seamlessly.

Limitations

This capability is not functional with the Point of Sale (PoS) app, as its stock interaction differs.

We advise using kits in such cases or performing regular inventory updates.
