Overview

About the industry

This industry vertical is engineered for campsite operators, RV park managers, and Odoo partners seeking a native solution to run outdoor hospitality enterprises without relying on fragmented reservation software, disconnected POS terminals, or paper pitch maps. It eliminates the severe operational friction caused by managing hybrid inventory, from bare tent pitches and utility-connected RV slots to fixed mobile homes, alongside high-volume seasonal check-in bottlenecks, uncaptured on-site retail consumption, and complex municipal tourist tax calculations. By extending core Odoo relational data models, the module seamlessly embeds spatial pitch allocation, dynamic stay duration, and equipment rental availability directly into native Rental, and POS workflows. Ultimately, it unifies housekeeping and reception operations with centralized financial governance, enabling real-time folio consolidation, automated utility billing, and streamlined multi-journal accounting directly within Odoo Sales, Invoicing, and Accounting.

What is an Odoo Industry Package?

Odoo Industry packages are ready-to-go databases built by Odoo S.A. for specific business sectors. They come pre-configured with the right apps, settings, and data to save you serious setup time.

If standard Odoo features don't fully cover an industry's unique needs, extra information or capabilities can be added directly to the industry module using Odoo Studio or custom lines of code. (Please check our pricing section to see which plan applies to this module!)

What's Included?

  • Step-by-step guides inside the Knowledge app to walk you through the system and explain how to configure and use your database.

  • Pre-set configurations so you don't have to start from scratch (including the right apps and settings).

  • Optional demo data to help you visualize how your workflows will look.

Pro Tip: Test the package with demo data on a separate test database first. Once you're comfortable with how it works, install a clean version without demo data on your live system.

Three Quick Things to Note:

1

Accounting is separate: It is never preinstalled out of the box, but you can easily add it whenever you're ready.

2

Inventory is kept basic: We purposely keep inventory settings simple at the start. It makes Odoo much easier to learn, and you can always turn on advanced tracking later.

3

Global scope: Industry modules are designed for worldwide use, meaning specific country-based localization needs are not covered out of the box.

Customization

Interactive Shift Gantt View for Pitches & Rentals

Streamline pitch allocation and rental accommodation scheduling through an interactive Gantt interface that supports direct creation and modification of rental orders.

  • Direct Order Manipulation: Create, update, extend, or shift camper reservations directly within the Gantt view while automatically updating underlying Sales Orders.

  • Visual Resource Scheduling: Drag-and-drop allocations across physical bare pitches, RV slots, and mobile homes with real-time validation against double-bookings and dimension constraints.

  • Contextual Quick-Actions: Access reservation details, check-in statuses and folio links directly via popover modals on active Gantt stay slots.

Resource-Bound Product Form Customization

Simplify product setup by directly linking physical pitch numbers and rental units while abstracting backend role and shift bindings from everyday configuration views.

  • Direct Resource Mapping: Bind physical campsite pitches, utility hookup points, and mobile home units directly on the product form for intuitive inventory management.

  • Role Abstraction: Hide complex role-to-shift technical bindings to present a clean, operational setup tailored for campsite reception administrators.

  • Automated Variant Sync: Map pitch attributes (electricity amperage, shade coverage, dimensions) directly to sellable product variants and booking rules.

Automated Tourist Tax Engine & Night Calculation Widget

Ensure regulatory compliance during check-out using an automated tourist tax calculation workflow powered by a dedicated stay-night and occupant widget.

  • Night & Occupant Calculation Widget: Automatically compute the exact billable night count directly on the order based on rental period and guest headcount attributes (adults, children).

  • Automated Tax Insertion: Append local municipal tourist tax lines to the order automatically at check-out to guarantee accurate folio invoicing.

Cross-Outlet POS Pitch Charge Payment Method

Enable campsite bars, snack shops, mini-markets, and equipment rentals to post charges straight onto a guest's active pitch order directly from the Point of Sale interface.

  • Dedicated Pitch Charge Method: Select the checkout payment method in POS to search active pitch numbers or camper names and instantly route bills.

  • Resource-Referenced Posting: Automatically reference the specific physical pitch or mobile home unit on consumption lines for clear, auditable guest folios.

  • Live Stay Verification: Validate active stay statuses and wristband/credit thresholds in real time before approving cross-outlet charge postings.

Daily Shift-Driven Turnover & Housekeeping Automation

Automate outdoor site logistics and accommodation turnovers by dynamically generating daily cleaning tasks based on staff shift schedules.

  • Daily Task Generation: Automatically trigger turnover tasks every morning for mobile home cleanings and pitch readiness checks.

  • Shift-Aware Assignment: Distribute task loads automatically among on-duty housekeeping staff members aligned with active shift schedules.

  • Credit-Based Workload Balancing: Assign point values based on unit types (e.g., full mobile home deep clean vs. bare pitch inspection) to balance shift workloads.

  • Real-Time Readiness Sync: Update pitch and rental unit operational states (Dirty, Clean, Ready) on reception views instantly upon task completion.

Automated Customer Satisfaction Survey Engine

Capture camper feedback automatically post-checkout to drive continuous park improvement and safeguard online reputation.

  • Triggered Survey Dispatch: Automatically send personalized satisfaction surveys via email following guest check-out.

Time-Horizon Steering View

Gain visual control over campsite sector allocations and future pitch availability across rolling seasonal time horizons.

  • Availability Timeline Matrix: Visualize real-time pitch availability across days, weeks, or months within a centralized steering interface.

  • Capacity Optimization: Identify allocation gaps between peak weekend arrivals to optimize pitch yield and adjust marketing strategy.

Executive Dashboard

Monitor critical outdoor hospitality metrics—including RevPAP (Revenue Per Available Pitch), ADR, and Occupancy—through native visual dashboards.

  • Key Yield Indicators: Calculate and display RevPAP, Average Daily Pitch Rate (ADR), and Occupancy Rate dynamically using total physical campsite capacity.

  • Performance Tracking Cards: Access real-time operational KPIs, booking pace trends, and ancillary retail sales summaries directly within a tailored management view.
