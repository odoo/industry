Overview

About the industry

This industry vertical is engineered for general contractors, project developers, and Odoo partners seeking a native solution to orchestrate complex construction operations without relying on disconnected spreadsheets or generic task management tools. It eliminates the operational friction caused by fragmented estimating, unpredictable material supply chains, unbilled contract variations, and the gap between back-office planning and job site execution. By extending core Odoo relational data models, the module seamlessly embeds structural project scopes into sales and project workflows to drive aligned procurement and site logistics. Ultimately, it unifies physical site execution with financial governance, enabling real-time cost tracking, contract variation control, and certified progress billing directly within Odoo Sales, Purchase, Inventory, and Invoicing.

What is an Odoo Industry Package?

Odoo Industry packages are ready-to-go databases built by Odoo S.A. for specific business sectors. They come pre-configured with the right apps, settings, and data to save you serious setup time.

If standard Odoo features don't fully cover an industry's unique needs, extra information or capabilities can be added directly to the industry module using Odoo Studio or custom lines of code. (Please check our pricing section to see which plan applies to this module!)

What's Included?

  • Step-by-step guides inside the Knowledge app to walk you through the system and explain how to configure and use your database.

  • Pre-set configurations so you don't have to start from scratch (including the right apps and settings).

  • Optional demo data to help you visualize how your workflows will look.

Pro Tip: Test the package with demo data on a separate test database first. Once you're comfortable with how it works, install a clean version without demo data on your live system.

Three Quick Things to Note:

1

Accounting is separate: It is never preinstalled out of the box, but you can easily add it whenever you're ready.

2

Inventory is kept basic: We purposely keep inventory settings simple at the start. It makes Odoo much easier to learn, and you can always turn on advanced tracking later.

3

Global scope: Industry modules are designed for worldwide use, meaning specific country-based localization needs are not covered out of the box.

Customization

BoM-Driven Quotation Scalability & Price Wizard

Scale complex material and labor assemblies using Bills of Materials on quotes, paired with an interactive wizard to update unit pricing on the fly.

  • Bill of Materials Exploding: Leverage structural BoMs directly inside quotation lines to automatically calculate all required raw materials and sub-assemblies.

  • Price Update Wizard: Update pricing dynamically using a dedicated wizard when component costs shift.

Multi-Dimensional Cost Nature Breakdown

Gain immediate visual visibility into how your project estimates and quotes break down across financial categories.

  • Early Cost Category Insights: Access an instant breakdown of costs (Labor, Materials, Equipments) directly from quotations and projects.

  • Granular Drill-Down: Drill into product categories and individual components from the dedicated dashboard to identify where to optimize margins or increase competitiveness.

WBS Execution via Dedicated Manufacturing Views

Translate complex Work Breakdown Structures into structured, manageable site execution steps.

  • Manufacturing Order Mapping: Map multi-level WBS phases directly into tailored Manufacturing Order views to organize custom site fabrications and structural assemblies.

  • Phase Execution Tracking: Monitor production and assembly progress across project phases in real time to keep large sites running according to schedule.

Component Procurement

Consolidate material needs across your entire project scope into a centralized inventory management.

  • Aggregated Shopping List: View all required raw components and plan procurement.

  • Batched Vendor Ordering: Trigger consolidated Purchase Orders or direct-to-site Drop Shipping orders grouped by vendor and site address.

  • Schedule-Aligned Purchasing: Filter procurement needs by manufacturing order start dates to execute just-in-time deliveries and prevent job site congestion.

Automated Cost Indexing for Live Quoting

Protect your future quotation margins against market volatility and supplier price fluctuations.

  • Dynamic Price Updates: Automatically adjust standard product cost prices based on confirmed Purchase Orders and validated Vendor Bills.

  • Catalog Price Sync: Update product vendor lists automatically in the Purchase tab to reflect the latest cost and volume deals for upcoming estimates.

Dedicated Progress Management

Streamline manufactured product delivery through a dedicated progress interface.

  • Increment Tracking: Measure cumulative completion progress directly from projects to drive accurate subsequent invoicing.

Project Progress Dashboard

Capture project detailed progress through an interactive spreadsheet reporting dashboard.

  • Multi-Filter Analytics: Dynamic Odoo Sheet dashboards allowing real-time filtering by Sales Order and Project.

  • Change Order Integration: Account for contract extras and variation orders within the overall progress report to maintain overall visibility.

  • Customizable KPIs: Leverage built-in spreadsheet functionality to adjust reporting formulas to meet specific executive or stakeholder needs.

Project Remarks

Bridge the gap between job site operations and office management with centralized remark logs.

  • Site Update Logging: Capture remarks during calendar event and on-site updates directly from project view.

  • Centralized Issue Follow-Up: Track field observations within project management to ensure operational bottlenecks are addressed swiftly.
